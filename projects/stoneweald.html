StoneWeald

A living prehistoric world.

An evolving 3D survival experience shaped by exploration, cooperation and the choices made along the way.

Explore the concept ↘Development status ↘
Independent Game In Development
01 / THE IDEA

Before history,
there is discovery.

StoneWeald is being developed as a living prehistoric world: a place for exploring, surviving and interacting with nature and other communities.

Its direction centers on a world that evolves over time, where the player's actions can affect the experience.

A standalone game in development — not a Minecraft product.
02 / THE EXPERIENCE

An untamed world.
Many possibilities.

02 / SURVIVAL

03 / EVOLUTION

A Changing World

03 / VISUAL DIRECTION

Stylized nature.
Immersive worlds.

A hybrid low-poly visual direction, combining natural landscapes, faceted geometry and warm atmospheric lighting — deliberately distinct from the cubed look of Minecraft.
